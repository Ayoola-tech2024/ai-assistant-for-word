================================================================================
                    AI ASSISTANT FOR MICROSOFT WORD
               Your Free, Smart AI Coworker Inside Word
================================================================================

Welcome! This assistant lives right beside Microsoft Word to help you write,
format, clean, research, and edit documents in plain English.

No technical skills, coding, or Python knowledge needed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUICK START (HOW TO RUN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your document in Microsoft Word (e.g. Document1 or any .docx file).
2. Double-click "Start AI Assistant.cmd" OR "AIAssistantForWord.exe".
3. The Assistant window will pop up right beside your Word document!
4. Type what you want in plain English, click "Send", review the preview,
   and click "Yes, do it" — watch Word change instantly!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SAFETY FIRST: YOU ARE ALWAYS IN CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Word is NEVER touched automatically.
* Every change is first shown to you in the preview box.
* You decide: click "Yes, do it" to apply, or "No, cancel" to discard.
* Made a mistake? Click the "Undo" button anytime to revert the last change.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INSTANT OFFLINE TOOLS (NO INTERNET OR SETUP NEEDED!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These features run locally on your PC in milliseconds with 0 internet needed:

* 🌿 Scientific & Botanical Names:
  Type: "italicise botanical names and underline them"
  -> Automatically finds Latin binomial species and biological families 
     (e.g., Chiroptera, Homo sapiens, Desmodus rotundus) and styles them.

* 🧹 Clean Markdown & Placeholders:
  Type: "clean markdown and remove sharps and placeholders"
  -> Strips raw hashtags (#, ##), bold asterisks (**), and [Insert ...] tags.

* 🔤 Font Resizing & Formatting:
  Type: "reduce font size" OR "set font size to 12" OR "make text bigger"

* 📄 1-Click PDF Export:
  Click the "📄 PDF" button or type "save as pdf" to convert your Word document
  to a clean PDF and reveal it in Windows File Explorer.

* 📊 Reading Metrics & Stats:
  Click the "📊 Stats" button to see word counts, reading time, speaking pace,
  and readability grade level.

* 📑 Native Table of Contents:
  Type: "insert table of contents" to add a native clickable Word TOC.

* 🏷️ Watermarks:
  Type: "add watermark CONFIDENTIAL" or "add watermark DRAFT".

* 📐 Alignment & Layout:
  Type: "center align", "justify text", "switch to landscape", "double space".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AI WRITING & RESEARCH (100% FREE CLOUD ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For drafting content from scratch or searching the web, the assistant uses
free AI keys:

* On first launch, click "Keys" at the top:
  - Option A (Recommended): Click "Get Groq key (free)" — gives thousands of 
    free uses per day.
  - Option B: Click "Get Google key (free)" — gives 20 free uses per day.
* Paste your key into the box and click "Save keys". You only need to do this ONCE.

Examples of what to ask the AI:
- "Draft a professional 2-page project proposal with budget and deliverables"
- "Research the latest facts about renewable solar energy and summarize findings"
- "Rewrite the selected paragraph to sound more executive and concise"
- "Translate the selected text into fluent French / Spanish / German"
- "Review this document and add margin critique comments"


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HANDY SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Ctrl + Enter : Send your request
* Ctrl + Space : Toggle voice dictation (speak directly to Word!)
* "Snap" Button: Snaps the assistant window neatly beside your Word window


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: It says "Open document: (checking...)" or doesn't detect my document?
A: Make sure Microsoft Word is running with a document open, then click "Refresh".

Q: Word shows a pop-up dialog (like Save As or Find)?
A: Close the dialog in Word first, then click "Try again" in the assistant.

Q: Can I share this with my team or friends?
A: YES! Simply share the "AI-Assistant-for-Word-v1.0.zip" file. Anyone on Windows 
   can unzip it and run it immediately!
